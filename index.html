Transaction Advisory Services

Built purposefully for private targets generating $500K to $10M in EBITDA.

📊

Full-Scope Post-LOI Quality of Earnings (QoE)

The industry gold standard for buy-side and sell-side diligence. We rigorously test earnings reliability, identify EBITDA add-backs, and give lenders the confidence they require.

  • Normalized EBITDA & Pro-Forma Adjustments
  • Proof of Cash (Bank-to-Book Reconciliations)
  • Net Working Capital (NWC) Peg Recommendation
  • Debt and Debt-Like Item Schedules
  • Customer & Margin Concentration Analysis
⚡

QoE Lite / Pre-LOI Red-Flag Screening

Designed for searchers and independent sponsors facing tight bid deadlines or budget parameters before committing full diligence capital.

  • Rapid 3 to 5 business day delivery
  • High-level revenue sanity checks & cash corroboration
  • Evaluation of seller-proposed add-backs
  • Identification of deal-breakers before legal spend
🛠️

P&L Reconstruction & Cash Normalization

Many founder-owned businesses maintain messy, cash-basis, or commingled books. We reconstruct accrual-based statements from scratch.

  • Conversion of cash-basis records into GAAP-aligned P&Ls
  • Disentangling personal, affiliate, and non-operating costs
  • Restatement of inventory and accounts receivable balances
  • Clean ledger preparation for institutional underwriting
🤝

Post-Acquisition & Tax Diligence

We ensure continuous transaction support through close, protecting you against hidden tax liabilities and working capital disputes.

  • State nexus, franchise, and sales tax exposure assessments
  • Closing date balance sheet and working capital true-up support
  • Opening balance sheet formulation and Day-One transition
  • Co-investor & lender compliance readiness
